Chinook Painting LTD (Est. 1983)

Chinook Painting LTD archive — established by grandfather in 1983.

Painting isn't just a trade for us; it’s a family tradition. Back in 1983, my grandfather laid down the roots of quality craftsmanship in our community by founding Chinook Painting LTD. He built his business on a simple, uncompromising promise: treat every property with respect, use the best materials available, and never cut corners.

Today, Shade Painting Company is proud to pick up that torch. We combine the time-tested values, work ethic, and attention to detail passed down from my grandfather with modern techniques and top-tier products. We aren't just updating walls—we are honoring a legacy of local reliability, one brushstroke at a time.
